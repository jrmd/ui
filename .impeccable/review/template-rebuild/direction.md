User feedback: portfolio spacing looks bad; ecommerce feels empty. User supplied Tailwind Plus, Shadcn Studio, Shadcncraft, Shadcn Space, Shadcn UI Kit, Solace UI (especially liked), and Tailark as inspiration. This pass replaces the compositions rather than adding space to the rejected rows.

Portfolio: Experience mode. Preserve Alex Rivers and three illustrative projects. Compact 56/44 desktop introduction, a wide Field notes interface concept directly below, paired secondary projects, concise approach and contact ending. First viewport must show work at meaningful size, not a disconnected poster with a large empty text column. Instrument Sans from the current shared project. Sage paper and green ink. The named motion is a small upward movement of the interface illustration on card hover, disabled for reduced motion. All three projects, About and Contact must remain connected.

Storefront: Persuade/Operate. Preserve Objects, the lamp and its finish selection/cart/checkout behaviour; expand into three illustrative homewares products. A room photograph anchors the hero; four shoppable pieces immediately follow. Editorial lighting feature, category navigation, local demo newsletter and complete footer add substance. No fabricated reviews or company performance claims. Full product detail, filters, empty search, multi-product persistent cart, checkout validation. Original imagery generated with built-in imagegen, prompts embedded in source PNGs. Room image is an editorial scene, not a specification image.

Quality bar: useful density and tightly grouped content like the supplied kits, especially Solace's artwork-led composition and Tailwind commerce's complete shopping controls. Native 1440, actual user 1149, and mobile 390 viewport widths. No copied commercial source. No approved raster comp; code-led implementation.
